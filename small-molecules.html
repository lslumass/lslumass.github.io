Models / HyRes/iCon

iCon small molecules

Cells are full of metabolites that condensates concentrate or exclude, and that can in turn drive or regulate phase separation. To bring them into the HyRes/iCon framework, we built iConMapper, a machine-learning tool that maps any small molecule onto the HyRes/iConNA bead scheme, and used it to construct iConMetabolome, a growing library of coarse-grained metabolite models that run alongside proteins and nucleic acids in HyresBuilder.

Automated mapping

iConMapper

iConMapper reads a molecule as a graph of atoms and bonds and predicts both how its atoms group into coarse-grained beads and which iCon bead type each bead should be. It is the deep supervised graph partitioning model with type prediction (DSGPM-TP), retrained on about 1,000 metabolites from the Human Metabolome Database that we labeled by hand following the HyRes and iConNA conventions.

  • 0.86F1 score, bead mapping
  • 0.80F1 score, bead type
  • ~1,000hand-labeled training molecules

From SMILES to a simulation-ready model

  1. Map. iConMapper turns a SMILES string into a coarse-grained structure, mapping scheme, and bead types.
  2. Simulate. A 500 ns all-atom simulation of the molecule (GROMACS with OpenFF or GAFF2) provides reference distributions of bonds, angles, and dihedrals.
  3. Fit. Bonded parameters are fit to those distributions and written to a parameter file that HyresBuilder loads directly.

The pipeline is fully automated, but checking the mapping and the fitted distributions by eye is recommended before production runs.

python MappingPrediction.py --smiles "<SMILES>" --name ATP --labels

Library

iConMetabolome

The library currently holds 88 parameterized metabolites, from amino acid derivatives and nucleotides to cofactors and TCA-cycle intermediates, and new molecules are added as they are parameterized. Each is shown below with its residue name in HyresBuilder. Select a molecule to see its bead mapping, drawn in iConMapper’s style so that each colored region is one bead, along with its bead types.

In the HyRes/iCon paper, iConMetabolome reproduces ATP-driven phase separation of protamine, including both its lower and upper critical concentrations, and the experimental trend of how 15 metabolites partition into SH3/PRM condensates (r ≈ 0.89).
